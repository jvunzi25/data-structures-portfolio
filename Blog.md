### What is the meaning of data science to me?
My meaning in data science is a way to creatively use data by using stats, math, code, ect. It also represents real lives, choices, and communities. We don't use data to put human experiences into rigid equations. Instead, we use it to spot trends, solve problems, and see big-picture issues that are hard to catch up close. Whether it's looking at healthcare access, city traffic, or job markets, or something I find fun like predicting sports stats and who you should put your parlay on; data gives us a clearer picture of how it works. It helps us see how big systems impact individual people, which leads to smarter choices for communities and leaders. Using data about real people is a big responsibility. Data without a backstory can easily lead to wrong conclusions or feed into harmful stereotypes. That's why having context really matters using data. In this class I want to learn about building my resume and GitHub more. I also want to learn about how to build solid skills in coding, data analysis, and visualization, while also learning how to spot the limits of numbers.
